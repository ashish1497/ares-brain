Captured by hand from the program team's email + linked Google Doc/Sheet — this

**General rules (all written exams):** Open Book unless noted otherwise —
open book means exactly ONE A4 sheet, handwritten, one side only, no
printouts, no internet/AI in the room. Report 15 min early, doors close 10
min before start. Blue ballpen only, no pencil.
